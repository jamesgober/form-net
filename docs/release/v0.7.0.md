# form-net v0.7.0 - Hardening

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Compare:** [`v0.6.0...v0.7.0`](https://github.com/jamesgober/form-net/compare/v0.6.0...v0.7.0)

**Attacked, fuzzed for a day, soaked for four hours.** v0.7.0 runs form-net against what real clients send, against mutations of it, against another parser, and against every threat in the plan's threat model. Three readings where form-net and another reader could disagree about a body are now refused. The speed of 0.6 is kept.

## What changed

- **Bare line breaks before a delimiter.** `--boundary` after a lone CR or LF, or as the first bytes of a part's data, is refused as `BoundaryInContent`, and the writer refuses such data too. Readers that accept a lone line break before a delimiter see more parts than form-net, so a body like that is one an attacker can shape to mean two things (D-113).
- **Quoted-looking unquoted names.** An unquoted `name` or `filename` that starts or ends with `'` is refused as `MalformedParameter` (D-114).
- **Parameter case.** `name`, `filename`, and `filename*` written other than in lower case are refused as `MalformedParameter`. RFC 7578 readers take `NAME="x"` as the name `x`; exact-match readers, multer among them, take it as no name (D-116).
- **The delimiter search** anchors on a CR or LF followed by `-`, so one pass finds delimiters and bare line breaks. At a chunk's edges and after a miss it compares a word at a time, and a miss resumes at the byte that differed (D-117).

## Verification

- **A capture corpus** (`tests/corpus`, D-115): the bodies Chrome 155, Edge 155, Firefox 159, Safari 26, curl 8.5, Python requests 2.31, and Go 1.27 send for a fixed test page, captured by `tools/capture`, and each checked whole and at every split. Safari is captured on a GitHub macOS runner through safaridriver (D-119).
- **A WAFFLED-style mutation generator**: every mutation of every captured body is refused or reads exactly as the original.
- **A multer differential**: every disagreement triaged in `docs/SECURITY.md`; the three that mattered are D-113, D-114, and D-116.
- **An attack test for every threat**, T-1 to T-38 (T-29 is the fuzzing).
- **24 hours of fuzzing** on every one of the 12 targets, on this code: 15.3 billion runs, no crash, no memory-limit stop, no real timeout. Eight targets were stopped once by a false timeout when the machine's clock stepped back; each input replays in milliseconds and each target was resumed with its time counted, the timeout check on (D-118).
- **The 4-hour soak** (NFR-15): 55.5 million uploads through one store, a fifth of them dropped part way; no growth in heap, files, or handles, and nothing left at the end.
- Local gate on Windows and WSL Ubuntu, stable and 1.85; CI green on every platform before the tag.

## Performance

Every family still clears the bar of 20% faster than the best other Rust crate, with the one 25-byte search query level as in 0.6 (`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`, baseline `benches/baseline/2026-10-30`):

- urlencoded to pairs, against form_urlencoded: 1.34x to 8.00x.
- urlencoded to a struct: 1.75x. Serialize: 2.20x.
- multipart streaming, against multer: 1.23x to 12.32x at every chunk size, the adversarial shapes included. 1 MiB of CR bytes parses in 25 µs, down from 67 µs.
- multipart buffered: 1.31x to 2.27x. To disk: 2.06x.
